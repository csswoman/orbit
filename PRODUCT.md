# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Orbit is a private, single-user life-organization app for someone managing many
different kinds of personal context: game events, food, subscriptions, things
they want, clothing, travel, sales, side projects, and visual inspiration. The
primary workflow is a short daily check of what needs attention and what useful
idea has been forgotten.

## Product Purpose

Orbit keeps time-sensitive commitments visible and deliberately resurfaces old
projects and inspiration. Success means the user can understand the next seven
days quickly, rediscover neglected ideas, and organize each life area without
forcing everything into a generic task model.

## Brand Personality

Personal, cosmic, and polished. The product should feel like a configurable
private universe rather than workplace software. The final visual identity will
be defined after the core product flows are working.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate task managers with interchangeable card dashboards.
- Gamer interfaces that turn organization into noisy gamification.
- Decorative cosmic effects that reduce readability or obscure real content.
- Rigid themes that require component rewrites when the user changes colors or
  chooses a dashboard background image.

## Design Principles

1. Upcoming commitments and resurfaced ideas are the home screen's clear center.
2. Preserve the differences between life spaces instead of flattening them into
   tasks.
3. Make visual identity configurable through semantic tokens and user settings.
4. Keep customization expressive but keep information legible over every theme
   and background.
5. Prefer calm, familiar product interactions; personality belongs in the
   visual world, not in surprising controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation, and
reduced-motion support. User-selected colors and background images must retain a
readable content layer and cannot be the only carrier of meaning.
